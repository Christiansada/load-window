# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Critique ideas and evidence rather than people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and publishing private information are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation, using proportionate action and an explanation where appropriate. Report concerns through GitHub's reporting tools or a private contact method the maintainer makes available. Do not include sensitive details in public issues.
