SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE

What this model includes

One 24-hour day, up to three loads, one-hour slots, constant base load, and energy-only rates. Every allowed start combination is checked. Equal-cost schedules choose the earliest start for the first load, then the next.

Cost includes base load. The power cap is an hourly model constraint, not an electrical safety rating. No startup surges, losses, variable cycles, taxes, demand charges, solar, or daylight-saving days. Results are conditional on your assumptions, not a bill forecast.
